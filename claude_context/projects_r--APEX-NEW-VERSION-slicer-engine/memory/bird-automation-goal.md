Goal (stated by the user, 2026-09-21): automate the manual iteration schedule of Joshua Bird's S4-Slicer deformation (research/S4_Slicer/, gitignored). Validated manual barème: ROTATION_MULTIPLIER=1, MAX_OVERHANG 30 → 20 → 10 → 5, one pass each.

User's own loop design (2026-09-26): deform → if fold, adjust and retry → if no fold and overhang above threshold, deform again → else finish. Output route agreed: no Cura / no retriangulation; the deformed height becomes the scalar field φ fed to Apex step5→step10 (Bird replaces steps 2-4). Not yet tested.

Stop criterion — convergence (user correction: they stopped at pass 4 because pass 5 changed nothing, not because of a fold). Benchy median node displacement ~1.1-1.8 mm for passes 1-4, then 0.004 mm. NOT reproduced on fine real_overhang (still 0.3-0.9 mm/pass after 9 passes). Caveat: bird_core.export_surface_stl recentres the tet in place, so detect_folds series continue from a recentred mesh while server.py continues from the raw one, and per-pass displacements include a rigid shift.

Fold judge (2026-09-26). The user's definition, from their drawing: a bent U is fine, a bar whose surface passes through itself is a fold.
- Rejected judges: flipped tets and overlaps between non-neighbour tets (catch normal wall compression); median crossing depth (the user's real fold, roof merging into cabin, is 0.089 mm, like healthy wrinkles at 0.07 mm; the earlier "0.52-1.76 mm folds" were coarse-mesh artefacts of real_overhang at 48 tets); geodesic distance between crossing faces (only a ×4 gap).
- Current best: surface self-intersection (step1_ingest._find_self_intersecting_faces, Apex venv, APEX_USE_CPP=1), grouped into connected zones, judged by the AREA OF THE LARGEST ZONE. Healthy benchy passes 3-5: 6-13 mm². User-made folds: hull folding on itself 69.7 mm² (ui_serie2_coque), roof merging into cabin 93.9 mm² (ui_serie1_toit_cabine pass 2), extreme 593 mm² (pass 3). Ranking matches the user's severity order. Proposed threshold ≈30 mm² (×5 margin to the smallest fold). Implemented as research/S4_Slicer/fold_judge.py (crossing_zones). Only one model so far: the mm² threshold may need to be relative to part or mesh size; check on death.stl. Series backed up in research/S4_Slicer/output_models/ui_serie*/. server.py now logs params per iteration (restart needed).
- Always deform on a fine mesh (benchy default 33k tets is fine; real_overhang needs max_tet_volume≈0.5).

Loop tool: research/S4_Slicer/bird_loop.py (written 2026-09-26). User rule: MAX_OVERHANG decreases (30,20,10,5,0) while passes change the part significantly; ROTATION_MULTIPLIER +0.5 when a pass barely changes it (median move < 25% of the largest seen — a value fitted on real_overhang's plateau, not on the user's judgement); schedule exhausted but still moving → repeat the same pass; one parameter per pass; a fold rejects the pass and backs off that parameter to the midpoint; stop on fold at minimal step, multiplier 4, or 16 passes.
